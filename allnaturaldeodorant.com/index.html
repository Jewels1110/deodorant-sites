03 / Sweat vs. smell

Sweat doesn’t smell. Bacteria do.

Antiperspirant and deodorant solve different problems. Knowing which is which explains almost everything about switching.

Antiperspirant

Blocks the sweat duct

Aluminum compounds physically plug the sweat duct. When you stop using it, those ducts take time to clear. That’s the transition period.

Natural deodorant

Lets you sweat, neutralizes odor

Your body sweats as it’s meant to. Sweat itself is odorless; baking soda (or arrowroot), coconut oil and tea tree target the bacteria that make it smell.

04 / The first 30 days

What the first 30 days really look like.

Switching from antiperspirant comes with a 1 to 4 week adjustment while your pores clear and rebalance. It’s temporary, and knowing it’s coming makes it easy.

  1. Day 1

    Switch

    Apply a thin, even layer to clean, dry underarms and give it a few minutes to absorb before dressing.

  2. Week 1 to 2

    Adjust

    As ducts clear you may sweat more and notice odor. Reapply midday, drink water and wear breathable fabrics.

  3. Week 2 to 3

    Stay with it

    This is the stretch that catches people off guard. It’s the transition, not the deodorant failing.

  4. Week 4 and on

    Balance

    Most people find odor is well managed once the transition passes, without anything plugging their pores.

08 / Questions

Ingredient questions, answered.

Will natural deodorant stop me sweating?

No. It controls odor, not wetness. Only antiperspirant stops sweat, by plugging the ducts. Sweat is a healthy body function, and on its own it doesn’t smell.

Why does baking soda irritate some people?

Baking soda works by raising the skin’s pH, which makes it harder for odor-causing bacteria to grow. Some skin reacts to that shift with redness or a rash, which is why the Baking Soda-Free formula uses cornstarch and arrowroot instead.

I’m sensitive to fragrance. Which scent should I choose?

Every scent uses essential oils, never synthetic fragrance. If you’re fragrance-sensitive, choose Unscented. Wait 15 to 30 minutes after shaving, since essential oils can sting freshly shaved skin.
